Silva

a forest of things — a commonplace book arranged by affinity, not filing

Silva is a private place to keep passages, thoughts, questions and overheard lines — and, more importantly, a place that keeps noticing them for you. It never files anything for you and never explains what it finds. It only ever puts two things beside each other, once in a while, and lets you decide whether it's onto something.

Understory — capture without commitment

Type or paste something worth keeping — that's the whole intake form, on purpose. Nothing else is required: no source, no category, no tags. It lands in the understory, where it sits for a season. Each day, decide: Keep it into the forest, or Release it. Ignore it entirely and it simply fades out on its own once its season ends — a backlog that ages out instead of piling up.

You can also import highlights from a Kobo e-reader — pick a KoboReader.sqlite file (copied off the device over USB) and Silva reads your highlights straight out of it, matching them to sources automatically and skipping anything already imported.

Forest — the kept collection

Every kept thing, one at a time, typeset like a specimen on a plate rather than a row in a table. Tap Edit on anything to set its locator (a page, a timestamp, "overheard on the 32 tram"), its Kind, a note, or a link — none of which you were ever asked for at capture. A Suggest a kind button offers a guess (a question mark → Question, quoted speech → Dialogue, and so on) — it's only ever a starting point you can accept, change, or ignore; Silva never assigns a kind on its own.

Clearings — patterns you name

A clearing (Loci in Notion) is a pattern you noticed across a few kept things — coined after the fact, never assigned automatically or at capture. From a clearing: add or remove things, rename it, combine two clearings into one, or split some of its members off into a new one when it turns out to be two patterns wearing one name.

Underground — the connections you've made

Walk a path between two kept things and say why — the "why" is the point of the record, not the connection itself. Every path you've walked, plus every kept thing, renders as an actual graph below the list: clustered by clearing, connected by solid lines.

Look closely and you may also see faint, dashed oxblood threads — mycorrhiza. These are things Silva noticed are quietly similar but you never explicitly connected. They're not clickable from the graph; the only way to act on one is if it surfaces as a provocation (below). When you see that colour, you're looking at something the app noticed and you haven't.

Search — by meaning, not just words

One box does both plain text matching and semantic search — find "a canine waiting patiently" and it can surface "a dog sat at the tram stop," even with no words in common. The semantic side runs a small model entirely on your device (a one-time ~25 MB download, cached after); nothing you search is ever sent anywhere. Filter by Kind to browse a single facet — Questions only, Dialogue only — with or without a query.

Provocations — the app's only voice

At most once a session, Silva offers a small nudge: something forgotten, two things placed side by side, a clearing that might be quietly forming. It never explains why — you decide whether it's a real connection, and if you accept one, you write the Why yourself. Dismiss it and it won't come back; it stays quiet the rest of the session either way.

One kind, Tension — two kept things that seem to pull in different directions — needs a real judgment call a similarity score can't make on its own, so it only appears once an Anthropic key is set in Settings. Every other kind works with nothing configured at all.

Demo mode, or your own Notion

Silva runs fully on a small set of sample things with no setup at all — everything above works the same way, just on data that resets if you clear your browser. To keep things for real, connect your own Notion workspace from Settings.

2 · Create an integration & connect

An internal integration is just a key that lets Silva reach your databases.

  1. Create it. Go to notion.so/my-integrations → New integration. Name it (e.g. Silva), pick the workspace your four databases live in, leave the type Internal, and save.
  2. Copy the token. On the integration's page, open Configuration, find the Internal Integration Secret, Show then Copy. Treat it like a password.
  3. Share all four databases with it. Open each one in Notion → top-right ••• → Connections → search your integration → select it. Without this, Silva sees nothing for that database.
  4. Paste the token into Silva. Open Silva → Settings → paste it into Notion token, then Test connection.

Your token (and, if you set one, your Anthropic key) is kept only in this browser and relayed to Notion/Anthropic through this site's proxy — never stored on a server.
