Fit Check

what should you wear today?

Fit Check is a little wardrobe helper. Take a photo of something you own, it figures out what kind of thing it is, and every day it puts together a few outfits from what you actually have — matched to the weather and however you're feeling. Tap the one you're wearing, or tell it "not today." That's really the whole app.

The four tabs

TodayYour outfit ideas for right now
WardrobeEverything you own
HistoryWhat you've worn (and skipped)
SettingsThe behind-the-scenes stuff

Settings is the boring one — that's where the grown-up setup lives, not anything you'll need day to day.

Adding something to your wardrobe

From Wardrobe, tap Add something and take a photo.

  • One item at a time works best — not a whole outfit in one shot.
  • A plain background helps, and good light matters more than a good camera.

Once you've taken the photo, Fit Check has a go at tagging it on its own — what kind of thing it is, its colours, how warm it is, when you'd wear it. It gets this right most of the time. When it doesn't, just tap the chips to fix it — nothing to type, nothing to get wrong. If you'd rather say the name out loud than type it, there's a little microphone next to the name box.

You'll also pick which wardrobe it belongs to — see below. You can pick more than one if it moves around with you.

Wardrobes

A wardrobe is just a place your clothes live — wherever you actually keep them. You can have as many as you want, name them whatever you actually call them, and switch one off if you don't need to see it for a while (nothing gets deleted, it just steps out of the way until you switch it back on). All of this lives in Settings.

Renaming a wardrobe is always safe, any time — nothing about your clothes changes, only what it's called.

Today's outfits

Open Today and Fit Check checks the weather where you are, then puts together three outfits from what you own. Pick how you want to feel — Comfy, Confident, Sporty, Cosy, Put-together, or Low-key — and it'll lean that way. Tap the same mood again to go back to no particular mood.

Every suggestion comes with one line explaining why it picked that — so it's never just a black box guessing at you. Found one you're wearing? Tap Wore this. Not feeling any of them? Not today just means today — nothing is ever held against a piece of clothing forever, it'll happily come back around.

History & favourites

History is a running list of everything you've worn or skipped, with the date, the weather, and your mood at the time. Tap the ♥ on any entry to mark it a favourite. You can also star any item in your wardrobe the same way — tap the star on its tile.

Light or dark

Fit Check follows your phone's light/dark setting automatically. If you'd rather pick one yourself, that's in Settings too, under Appearance.

The grown-up part

Where your stuff is actually stored

Everything you add — your clothes, your wardrobes, your outfit history — lives in a Notion workspace, not on some company's server. Nothing is collected, nothing is sold, there's no account to make. Someone in your family set this connection up already, so you shouldn't need to touch any of this.

For whoever manages the Notion side: token and database links are entered once in Settings and stay only on this device — sent straight to Notion, never through a server that stores them. A ready-made Starter Template has the three databases (Garments, Wardrobes, Outfits) already cross-linked, correctly set up — duplicate it into your own workspace, share each database with your Notion integration, then paste the token and the three database links into Settings and tap Test connection. Without a token, the app runs on realistic demo data and stays fully explorable — including trying out wardrobe and garment management — with nothing saved anywhere once you close the tab.
