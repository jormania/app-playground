Google Health link

Lets Yoru and Touch Grass read your Fitbit's sleep, steps and heart rate. Read-only, and nothing is stored on the server but the connection itself.

This device

Every app on this site reads the same saved key, so this is once per browser.

Google

Save the key to check the connection.

Google will warn that the app is unverified. That's expected for a personal project with one user: choose Advanced → Go to …, then allow the three read-only permissions.

Claude

  1. In Claude, open Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector.
  2. Name it Google Health and paste the URL:
    https://coneofcold.vercel.app/api/health-mcp/<GOOGLE_HEALTH_MCP_KEY>
  3. Leave the OAuth fields empty. The key in the URL is the password, so keep the URL to yourself.
