Lexi5

five letters, six chances.

Lexi5 is a daily word puzzle. You have six tries to guess a hidden 5-letter word. After each guess, the colour of the tiles will change to show how close your guess was to the word.

How to play

W Green: The letter is in the word and in the correct spot.

E Yellow: The letter is in the word but in the wrong spot.

A Gray: The letter is not in the word in any spot.

Dictionaries & Difficulty

Lexi5 comes with three different dictionary tiers that drastically change the difficulty and vocabulary pool. You can switch between them in the Settings menu.

  • Standard: Uses the official curated list of ~2,300 common English words. If you want the traditional experience, stick here.
  • Expanded: Uses the Stanford GraphBase list of ~5,700 words. You'll encounter more obscure vocabulary and plural nouns.
  • Expert: Uses the massive list of ~13,100 words originally allowed as guesses. This mode is extremely challenging and frequently results in highly unusual answers.

Lexi5 automatically fetches the dictionary definition of the winning word and displays it on the stats screen — perfect for when you finally guess an obscure word in Expert mode!

Hard Mode

Turning on Hard Mode forces you to use any revealed hints in subsequent guesses. For example, if you find a green 'A' in the second spot, every following guess must have an 'A' in the second spot. It stops you from throwing out a completely unrelated word just to eliminate vowels.

Stats & Unlimited Play

Your statistics (games played, win percentage, and guess distribution) are tracked separately for each dictionary. A 100% win rate on Standard won't be ruined if you experiment with Expert mode!

Unlike the original game, once you finish the daily word, you can tap Play Again to keep going. Lexi5 deterministically creates a new word for every game you play in a day, meaning you can play infinitely without ever running out. You can also use the Share button to send a specific "seed link" to a friend so they can play the exact same grid you just solved.
